\section[Deep-learned scatter initialisation]{Deep-Learned Scatter Initialisation for \glsentryshort{sirf}}
\label{app:scatter_dl}

The \href{https://github.com/samdporter/spect-scatter-deep-learning}{adapted scatter-estimation repository} integrates the pre-trained network of Xiang et al.\ \cite{xiangDeepNeuralNetwork2020} into \gls{sirf} through configuration and inference scripts. The network architecture and training belong to the original authors; the contribution here is the \glsentryshort{sirf} inference path. Because the training and acquisition protocols differ, its output serves only as a heuristic initialisation for the non-primary estimate, which is refined and frozen as described in Section~\ref{subsec:additive_refinement}.
